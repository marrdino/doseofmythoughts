Who we are

A Dose of My Thoughts is a YouTube channel run by Rahdevs, Norway. This policy covers this website and the channel's in-house tool "YouTube Studio Rahdevs".

This website

This website has no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. We do not collect personal data from visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Our use of YouTube API Services

"YouTube Studio Rahdevs" uses YouTube API Services. It is used only by the channel owner, with the owner's own Google account, to:

  • upload, update and publish videos on our own channel;
  • read our own channel's statistics (views, watch time, audience retention, click-through rate and subscriber changes) to learn which videos work.

The tool does not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users or viewers. Statistics are aggregated numbers from YouTube Analytics. They are stored on the owner's own computer, refreshed or deleted within 30 days as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies, and never sold or shared.

By watching our videos you are using YouTube, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Revoking access

Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on our YouTube channel.
